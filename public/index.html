    Questions

    Are my images uploaded?

    No. The images are compressed by your own browser and never leave your device. Nothing is saved, and anything held while working is deleted when you close the tab.

    How much smaller will my images get?

    Photos usually shrink by 50 to 80 percent at quality 75 with little visible change. Saving as WebP often gives the smallest files.

    Is there a limit?

    You can compress up to 100 images at a time, each up to 40 MB. It is free, with no watermark and no sign-up.
